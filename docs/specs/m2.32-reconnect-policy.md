# M2.32 — Connection Backoff & Reconnect Policy

## Objective

Add deterministic reconnect policy and scheduling without putting backoff behavior into the M2.31 lifecycle state machine.

## Boundaries

### ReconnectPolicy

Calculates delay and exhaustion from an attempt number.

Supported strategies:

- exponential
- linear
- constant

Optional jitter is applied at the policy boundary:

- none
- full
- equal

### ReconnectScheduler

Turns a calculated delay into waiting. The scheduler owns time; the policy does not.

### ReconnectExecutor

Owns the retry loop around a transport `connect()` operation.

It:

1. asks the scheduler for the current attempt delay
2. calls transport connect
3. resets the policy after a successful connection
4. stops when the policy is exhausted

## Invariants

- Delay calculation is deterministic when jitter is `none`.
- Policy does not perform I/O.
- Scheduler does not decide whether a retry is allowed.
- Executor does not own lifecycle state.
- Successful connection resets attempt accounting.
- Exhausted reconnect attempts produce a clean failed result.

## M2.31 integration boundary

M2.31 remains responsible for:

`DISCONNECTED → RECONNECTING → RESYNCING`

M2.32 is responsible only for obtaining a viable transport connection under a retry policy.

An upstream adapter may inject `ReconnectExecutor` into the M2.31 recovery coordinator in a future milestone. No direct coupling is introduced here.

## Non-goals

- classifying close reasons
- deciding whether reauthentication is required
- Signal/session repair
- stream bootstrap
- message replay ordering
