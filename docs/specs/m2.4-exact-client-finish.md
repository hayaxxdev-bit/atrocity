# M2.4 — Exact WhatsApp ClientFinish

Current Baileys constructs ClientFinish after `processHandshake()` produces
the encrypted client static (`keyEnc`) and the client payload is encrypted
through the Noise handler. The resulting handshake message contains
`clientFinish.static` and `clientFinish.payload`, then gets encoded and sent
through the Noise transport.

Reference:
https://github.com/WhiskeySockets/Baileys/blob/master/src/Socket/socket.ts

Atrocity provides:
- `buildWhatsAppClientFinish()`
- `WhatsAppClientFinish`
- `composeWhatsAppClientFinish()`

The builder owns structure/invariants. Encryption and wire serialization are
injected dependencies.

No exact wire bytes are fabricated until a captured/reference WAProto vector
is available.
